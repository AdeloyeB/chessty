# Naming Research: Chess Betting App

This document explores naming options for your chess betting platform. The goal: a name that feels **Gen Z**, **Polymarket-esque**, works globally, and is available as `.xyz` or `.com`.

---

## What Makes Polymarket-Style Names Work

### The Polymarket Story
Polymarket was originally called **Union.market**, then rebranded. The "Poly-" prefix (Greek for "many") signals "many markets." Simple, descriptive, memorable.

**Kalshi** — their main competitor — means "everything" in Arabic. It's short (6 letters), exotic-sounding but pronounceable, and conveys broad scope.

### Gen Z Naming Trends (2025-2026)

| Trend | Examples | Why It Works |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| **Ultra-short (4-6 letters)** | Uber, Zoom, Bolt, Snap | Easy to type, remember, say |
| **Single abstract words** | Stripe, Square, Block | Clean, ownable, brandable |
| **Invented compound words** | Polymarket, Coinbase | Descriptive but unique |
| **Dropped vowels** | Tumblr, Flickr, Grindr | Internet-native, domain-friendly |
| **Tech-chic endings** | -ix, -on, -a, -en, -ra | Lumet, Nuvio, Axiom |
| **X, Z, V sounds** | Zelle, Venmo, Nexo | Futuristic, high-velocity feel |

### .xyz Domain Landscape

- **Why .xyz**: Became the go-to for Web3/crypto. Google uses abc.xyz. ENS, Axiom, Mirror all use .xyz. Signals forward-thinking.
- **Tempo.xyz sold for $149,888** — short .xyz domains have real value
- **.com still king** for mainstream trust, but .xyz is credible for tech-native audiences

## Naming Directions

### Direction 1: Chess Terms (Direct)

Chess has incredible vocabulary. Many terms have double meanings that work for betting.

| Name | Meaning | Vibe | Domain Thoughts |
|------|---------|------|-----------------|
| **Gambit** | A sacrifice for advantage | Perfect — betting IS a gambit | gambit.xyz is for sale; gambit.com taken |
| **Tempo** | Each move = unit of time | Speed, momentum | tempo.xyz sold ($150K); tempo.com taken |
| **Blitz** | Fast chess (3-10 min games) | Speed, intensity | Worth checking |
| **Zugzwang** | Forced to move, all moves bad | Unique but hard to spell | Niche appeal only |
| **Castl** | Castle (defensive move) | Clean, vowel-dropped | Likely available |
| **En Passant** | "In passing" capture | Too long | Skip |

**Top pick from this direction: `Gambit`** — the word already means "calculated risk" in everyday English. Perfect for a betting platform.

### Direction 2: Ultra-Minimal (4-5 Letters)

The Uber/Zoom/Bolt approach. One punchy word.

| Name | Meaning | Vibe | Notes |
|------|---------|------|-------|
| **Movr** | Move + -r ending | Action-oriented | Dropped vowel, internet-native |
| **Stakd** | Staked | Betting/crypto crossover | Aggressive, memorable |
| **Checkd** | Checked | Chess + verification | Clean |
| **Bltzd** | Blitzed | Fast chess, intensity | Maybe too aggressive |
| **Wager** | To bet | Direct, English word | Classic |
| **Stake** | What you bet | Already a major crypto casino | Avoid |

---

### Direction 3: Number/Letter Codes

The a16z, x.com, 37signals approach. Insider-feeling, memorable.

| Name | Meaning | Vibe | Notes |
|------|---------|------|-------|
| **64** | 64 squares on a chessboard | Insider knowledge | 64.xyz worth checking |
| **e4** | Most common first move (pawn to e4) | Chess nerds will love | e4.xyz, e4.com likely taken |
| **d4** | Second most common opening | Same energy | d4.xyz |
| **m8** | "Mate" (checkmate) | Playful, Gen Z texting | m8.xyz could be gold |
| **1.e4** | Notation for first move | Too literal | Skip |

**Top pick from this direction: `m8`** — it's "mate" (checkmate) spelled like Gen Z texts. Short, playful, memorable. Works as "m8.xyz" or "playm8" etc.
